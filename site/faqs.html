Your painting

Can you create a portrait from just a photograph?

Yes. I specialise in transforming client photographs into bespoke house portraits. Just one clear photo of your property is all I need to create a one-of-a-kind painting. But additional images - taken close up or at different angles - will help me capture architectural details, textures, and the unique character of the building.

  • Photo quality: A high‑resolution image is ideal and will result in a more accurate painting, but I can work from older or slightly imperfect photos in some cases.
  • Personalisation: Specific details can either be emphasised or left out, at your request. For example, I can paint your favourite flower in full bloom, while removing the satellite dish.
  • Multiple photos: If you don't have a single photo that includes the whole property, I can work from multiple photo references, combining them to create a fuller, more balanced portrait.
  • Digital to physical: Your digital photograph is transformed into a piece of physical hand-painted artwork.

    • ‍

    Can I request specific details to be included in the painting?

    I welcome personalisation requests to make your house portrait truly unique.

    • Custom features: You can ask for favourite garden plants, or seasonal touches to be included.
    • Architectural details: Specific windows, doors, or decorative elements can be emphasised.
    • Personal memories: If you’d like a certain tree, car, or family detail added, I can weave it into the composition.
    • Stylistic choices: You can request a particular mood — for example, a sunny summer day or a cosy winter scene.
    • Multiple references: If you have more than one photo, I can combine them to capture the details you care about most.

    Do you send a sketch for approval?

    I always send a photo of my sketch for your approval before moving on to the ink and watercolour stage.

    Ordering & payment

    How long will my painting take?

    Please allow 2–3 weeks from confirming your commission to receiving your painting. I only take a limited number of bookings each month, so every piece gets my full attention.

    • Availability: When you send your request, I’ll reply by email to let you know my current availability.
    • Delivery: UK orders are sent by 48-hour tracked postage. International delivery takes longer and depends on your country’s customs rules, so please allow extra time.
    • Deadlines: If you need your painting by a certain date, such as for a special event, mention it in your request and get in touch as early as you can.

    How do I pay?

    Once we’ve confirmed the details of your painting together, your payment secures your booking.

    • Payment methods: I prefer bank transfer, but card payments through Stripe are also available.
    • No obligation: For anything outside my standard sizes, I’ll send you a quote first. There’s no pressure to place an order once you’ve received it.
    • Buying direct: You’re paying the artist, not a selling platform, so I only charge for your artwork and don’t need to cover platform fees.

    Delivery & framing

    Is my painting framed?

    No. Paintings are sent unframed, so you can choose a frame that suits your home and style. Each painting has a generous white border to give you more framing options.

    • Fits standard frames: For example, the A5 painting on A4 paper will sit happily in an A5 or A4 frame, depending on the look you want.
    • Straight edge: This can be framed with or without the white border showing.
    • Organic edge: The border becomes part of the painting, so this style works best framed with the white border included.

    Do you ship artwork outside the UK?

    Yes. I offer international shipping so clients worldwide can enjoy bespoke house portraits. Simply send me your commission request and we will discuss the delivery options available.

    • Shipping costs: Shipping prices vary depending on your location and the size of artwork; this will be confirmed before your order is finalised.
    • Delivery times: International shipping usually takes longer than UK delivery - and is subject to your country's unique customs regulations - so please allow extra time for your commission to arrive.
    • Secure packaging: Your artwork is carefully wrapped to protect against damage during transit.
    • Tracked delivery: International orders are sent using reliable courier services with tracking available.

    Gifts

    Can I commission a painting as a surprise gift?

    Yes. Many of my commissions are gifts, and they’re a thoughtful present people probably won’t expect.

    • Gift-wrapped as standard: Every painting is carefully gift-wrapped before being securely packaged, so unboxing becomes part of the experience.
    • Photos without asking: You don’t need perfect photos. I can work from older or slightly imperfect photos in some cases, or combine several photos into one portrait.
    • Sent straight to them: I can post the painting directly to the person you’re gifting it to. Just give me their delivery address when you book. Please note that I’m not able to include a gift note.
