About the Artist

I’m Esther. And, I know just how meaningful a bespoke gift can be - because I received one.

My grandpa once gave me a handwritten journal of his favourite poems; personalised with notes and pictures. I treasure it because of the connection to him.

~ The time. The thought. The handmade-ness.

My clients appreciate how meaningful art can be too. Artwork that takes time, is thoughtful, handmade, individual. Not off the shelf and found on a thousand other walls. Imagine how such a piece might be treasured!

I want to offer that kind of experience to my clients.

How This Works

Send Your Request

Tell me a bit about the painting you have in mind - using my contact form - and I’ll reply by email to let you know my availability.

Confirmation & Payment

We can confirm the details together and you can review the commission terms. When you’re ready, your payment (or deposit) secures your booking.

Painting

I bring your bespoke painting to life from your reference photos. I’ll send you a progress photo at the drawing stage for your approval.

Shipping

You can have a sneak peak (if you want) before it is gift-wrapped. Your artwork is then packaged securely and shipped using a tracked delivery service.

Materials

My Shop & Our Home

I don't want to choose between caring for my clients and caring for the planet. That’s why I’m making more conscious choices about how I produce, pack, & ship my art.

Recyclable Packaging

Locally recyclable, that is. You’ll find specific information inside your parcel.

Eco Paper

My art prints are available on Agave or Hemp paper. Very high quality, archival, giclee prints - but kinder on the planet.

Reused Packaging

Spot any plastic? I try to reuse items that come into my home instead of throwing them away.

Dropshipping

Your print order travels to you directly from my print provider, so that’s one journey, not two!
